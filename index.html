FAQ

Questions clients often ask

How much does a website cost?

Prices start at $35 for a landing page and $50 for a multi-page site. The final price depends on the number of pages and features, and you get a fixed quote before work begins.

How long does a project take?

A landing page usually takes a few days and a larger site one to two weeks, depending on how quickly I receive your content and feedback. I confirm the timeline in the quote.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and tested on phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Send me the link and tell me what you don’t like about it. I’ll suggest what to improve and quote the redesign.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. I fix bugs and make small updates after the site goes live. Just message me.
